\label{sec:s-posthoc}
The analyses in this section were specified after the primary results were known, committed (\texttt{configs/\allowbreak posthoc.json}) before they were run, and use the splits, inner folds, selection objective, checkpoint and threshold rules, cross-fitting recipe and final seeds of the primary protocol. No primary result was changed.
