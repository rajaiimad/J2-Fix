\section{Post-hoc analyses}
\label{sec:s-posthoc}
All analyses in this section were specified after the primary results were known. They use the splits, inner folds, selection objective, checkpoint and threshold rules, cross-fitting recipe and final seeds of the primary protocol, and no primary result was changed. They differ in when they were specified. The exposure-time baselines (S5.1), the Holm adjustment (S5.2), the comparison with always alarming (S5.3), the grouping sensitivity analyses (S5.4) and the cost curves (S5.5) were committed (\texttt{configs/\allowbreak posthoc.json}) before they were run. The paired comparisons of the exposure-time baselines with the primary models (main text, Section~V-C) and the Q-value scale (S5.6) were added while the results were written up, without prior commitment. Sections~S5.7--S5.10 were added after a pre-submission review, when all other results were known (key \texttt{review\_round\_2}); they use only the saved per-sample predictions, selected configurations and search logs. Section~S5.11 was prompted by the same review but was specified, with its decision rules, and committed before any of its models was trained; like the exposure-time baselines of S5.1, it trains new models under the full protocol. As in the main text (Section~III-G), bootstrap $p$-values between 0.01 and 0.05 in the tables of this section are approximate, and a difference whose confidence interval includes zero is a point estimate only.

\subsection{Search-draw sensitivity (specified before it was run)}
\label{sec:s-searchdraw}
Prompted by the dependence of E-DDQN's result on its single random draw (S5.10), this analysis was specified in \texttt{configs/\allowbreak posthoc.json} (key \texttt{search\_draw\_sensitivity}), together with its decision rules, and committed before any of its models was trained. E-DDQN and the standard DQN were each tuned again under the full protocol---same splits, inner folds, search training seed, budget of 16 configurations, selection rule, checkpoint rule, cross-fitting, five final seeds and threshold rules---with two further random draws from the full search space (configuration seeds 9120 and 9130 for E-DDQN, 9122 and 9132 for DQN) and with one draw of 16 configurations restricted to the exposure feature set (9200 and 9202). Perturbation variants were not scored. Draw~1 is the primary analysis and is reproduced exactly. Every variant is reported; none replaces the primary results. The decision rules were: (R1) the statement that E-DDQN does not outperform the standard DQN is challenged if, in any pair, E-DDQN's seed-ensemble cost is lower and the 95\,\% group-bootstrap interval of the difference excludes zero; (R2) E-DDQN's result is called draw-dependent if its seed-mean cost or its within-fold ROC-AUC spans more than 0.05 over its three full-space draws; (R3) under the exposure-only restriction, a within-fold ROC-AUC of E-DDQN within 0.03 of DQN's supports the feature-set explanation of Section~V-G (iii), one more than 0.05 below DQN's means that this explanation is insufficient, and anything in between is inconclusive.

Per variant: number of exposure-only candidates in the draw, number of outer folds whose selected configuration uses the questionnaire indicators (Q folds), 5:1 cost (rule D1; mean $\pm$ SD over seeds), seed-ensemble cost, pooled and within-fold ROC-AUC (seed means), and the discount factor selected in each outer fold.

\emph{Outcome under the pre-specified rules.} R1 (headline challenged): \SDROne{}; no pair gave E-DDQN a significantly lower cost (differences \SDCostDiffMin{} to \SDCostDiffMax; smallest group-bootstrap $p=$\,\SDCostPMin). R2 (draw-dependent): \SDRTwo{}; over its three full-space draws E-DDQN's seed-mean cost spans \SDCostSpan{} (\SDEDDQNCostMin--\SDEDDQNCostMax) and its within-fold ROC-AUC \SDWfSpan{} (\SDEDDQNWfMin--\SDEDDQNWfMax), against \SDDQNCostMin--\SDDQNCostMax{} and \SDDQNWfMin--\SDDQNWfMax{} for the standard DQN. R3 (feature-set explanation): \SDRThree; with the exposure features only, the standard DQN's within-fold ROC-AUC exceeds E-DDQN's by \SDExposureGap. Beyond the rules, the ROC-AUC differences show that the ranking of the two agents reverses between draws: the standard DQN ranks seconds better in draw~1 ($p=$\,\SDPairOneAucP, approximate) and E-DDQN in draw~2 ($p=$\,\SDPairTwoAucP). The variants were not compared with the other models by any test. Retraining a sample of the new search and final jobs from scratch reproduced their scores bit for bit (Section~S7).
