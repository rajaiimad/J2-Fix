\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:s-repro}
\begin{itemize}
\item Software: Python 3.13.16, PyTorch 2.14.1 (run on the CPU), scikit-learn 1.9.1, NumPy 2.5.3, pandas 3.0.5, SciPy 1.18.1, SHAP 0.53.0, statsmodels 0.15.0 (\texttt{requirements.txt}).
\item One command regenerates everything from the raw XML files: \texttt{bash run\_all.sh -{}-fresh} (about 2.5 to 3.5 hours of wall time on four CPU cores, dominated by the reinforcement-learning hyper-parameter searches and ablation). A clean rerun of commit \texttt{2e49e93} from an empty directory, including the search-draw analysis, took about 2 hours 46 minutes of computing and reproduced every per-sample prediction, every reported number and the text of the paper and supplement of that commit exactly; it was interrupted once by a restart of the computing container and resumed from its saved per-job outputs, as recorded in \texttt{results/\allowbreak qc/\allowbreak clean\_rerun.md}. Later commits changed the text and references, corrected the implementation of decision rule R3 of the search-draw analysis (its outputs are unchanged) and added the descriptive calculation of Section~S5.12. With the final code, the steps after model training were rerun in the same clean copy; they reproduced every result file of this version exactly, apart from wall-clock timing values that the text does not use (\texttt{results/\allowbreak qc/\allowbreak final\_version\_verification.md}).
\item The post-hoc analyses (Section~\ref{sec:s-posthoc}) are recorded in \texttt{configs/\allowbreak posthoc.json}; Section~\ref{sec:s-posthoc} states which of them were committed before they were run and which were added later. They are produced by \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 11\_posthoc.py} (\texttt{run} and \texttt{analyze}; seeds 801--802 for the post-hoc search, bootstrap seeds 5--7), except the score-scale calculation (Section~S5.12), produced by \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 14\_score\_scale.py}, and the search-draw sensitivity analysis (Section~S5.11, key \texttt{search\_draw\_sensitivity}), which is produced by \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 13\_search\_draw\_sensitivity.py} (configuration seeds 9120, 9130, 9122, 9132, 9200 and 9202; bootstrap seeds 5 and 6).
\item Seeds: split 20261012 (outer) and 20261013--20261017 (inner); hyper-parameter search 777 (+family index for configuration sampling); final training 1000--1004; perturbations 4242; bootstrap 11 (CIs) and 5 (paired tests).
\item Every number in the manuscript and in this supplement is produced by \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 05\_make\_tables.py} (primary analysis) from \texttt{results/\allowbreak tables/\allowbreak *.csv}, which \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 04\_aggregate.py} computes from the per-sample predictions in \texttt{results/\allowbreak predictions/\allowbreak }, or by \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 11\_posthoc.py}, \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 13\_search\_draw\_sensitivity.py} and \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 14\_score\_scale.py} (post-hoc analyses, Section~\ref{sec:s-posthoc}). \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 08\_consistency\_check.py} recomputes confusion matrices, metrics and McNemar statistics with independent library implementations and checks them against the manuscript macros.
\item Executable sanity tests of the corrected agent: \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 00\_sanity\_tests.py} (reward regret ratio, implied threshold, within-session transitions, terminal flags, reward/action consistency of replay, target-network buffer synchronisation, Q-values within 1.5 times the return bound on a toy problem, McNemar identity)); checks of the evaluation boundaries, the selection steps and the replay buffer on the real folds: \texttt{scripts/\allowbreak 15\_pipeline\_verification.py} (Section~\ref{sec:s-verify}).
\end{itemize}
